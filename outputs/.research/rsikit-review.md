Reviewed 2026-09-16 against `outputs/rsikit-improvement-modules-comparison.md`, the three supporting research notes, current RSIKit interfaces and packing integration, and the 16 population/operator implementations inspected directly during research. This is an evidence and architecture review, not a fresh benchmark or test run. No edits made to the comparison report.

The ordinary function/state approach matches existing RSIKit better than a new universal runtime.

## Checks and reasoning

- **Scope:** counted exactly 45 agent rows and 45 distinct implementation links in section 3. The four concrete RSIKit search strategies are separately mapped, avoiding double-counting. LMCA, EPO, zero-shot selector synthesis and EoT are explicitly identified as diagnostics/allocation/synthesis/inference donors rather than measured iterative improvement.
- **Current interface accuracy:** `SequentialStrategy.generate()` yields at most one pending candidate; `update()` requires exactly matching candidates/evaluations. `Candidate` has only one parent ID; the separate `selections` record retains multiple parents/inspirations. `Evaluation` contains validity, finite named metrics and textual feedback. The report correctly treats richer evidence as a future adapter or side table and reserves batch/concurrent recipes from single-pending inheritance.
- **Current population semantics:** RSIKit EoH freezes its evolutionary parent population until a complete five-operator cycle, while global best is updated immediately; initialization fills separately. RSIKit AlphaEvolve is serial and single-objective, preserving island/cell champions and periodically reseeding weaker islands. DGMArchive admits every valid child and discounts selection by admitted child count; it does not execute self-modification. All are represented accurately.
- **Proposer integration:** operation context currently crosses through a closure in the packing example, not the core `Propose` signature. The recommendation to generalize the existing operator/context composition, rather than claim it is already a universal proposer, is appropriate. Thought/diagnosis maps are example-owned. `RepairingProposer` enforces parent edit boundaries each time; the application reuses actual checked measurements. The report distinguishes these responsibilities correctly.
- **Critical innovations remain visible:** MEOH objective tradeoffs, EoH-S case complementarity, QD descriptors and PSRO opponent mixtures remain different evidence/selection semantics. Model-guided selection is separate from measured fitness. ReEvo reflection, persistent memory and retrieval remain separate. Soft-prompt/model training is distinct from textual guidance; reward-policy training remains evaluator work. Population, historical best and eligibility/retirement remain distinguishable.
- **Outlier scope:** numerical-vector optimization, game mixtures, PINSKY task/policy coevolution, PersonaEvolve distribution matching and LATS mutable environments require adapters/recipes. The report says they cannot be inserted unchanged into current single-source state. Keeping these out of the initial extraction avoids hiding their innovations behind scalar `fitness`.
- **Measurement claims:** the report labels build-order checks as future acceptance criteria, proposed combinations as hypotheses, primary correspondence as qualified, and existing tests as contract checks rather than experimental validation. I did not independently re-run the lead's cited 38-test baseline and make no additional test-result claim.

After the identified wording/link corrections and draft finalization, the report is suitable as the conceptual design input for RSIKit extraction. No additional framework abstraction is justified by this review.
