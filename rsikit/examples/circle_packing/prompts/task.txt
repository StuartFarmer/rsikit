Pack exactly ten circles in the unit square, maximizing the sum of their radii.
Implement pack_circles() returning a list of ten [x, y, radius] triples with
finite numeric entries. Every radius must be positive. All circles must fit
inside [0,1] x [0,1] without overlap. NumPy and SciPy are available in the
execution sandbox. Preserve the function interface and the protected source
outside evolution blocks. The fixed geometry evaluator determines validity and
measures sum_radii; do not change or bypass that evaluator.
